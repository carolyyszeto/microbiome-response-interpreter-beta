# Known limitations

- The workflow is an exploratory response-organization descriptor, not a validated clinical or diagnostic tool.
- Directional coherence depends on feature-space definition, endpoint alignment, zero handling, sample size, and group-direction stability.
- Pseudocount sensitivity checks compare specified settings only. They do not establish invariance across all zero-replacement strategies.
- The sample-size operating guide uses simplified simulations. It should not be interpreted as a formal trial power calculator or a minimum detectable effect guarantee.
- CAZyme or pathway-style feature spaces should be interpreted as gene-family or feature-space organization unless independently validated as enzyme activity, pathway activity, or metabolic flux.
- Toy datasets are for installation and output-shape checks only. They are not biological validation datasets.
