# Changelog v6.2

## Reader-Friendly Decision Flow Add-On

V6.2 adds a practical interpretation layer for users who need a simplified decision tree for magnitude-coherence outputs.

Added resources:

- `references/decision-flow.md`
- `scripts/decision_flow_summary.R`

The decision-flow layer translates four common response-geometry patterns into cautious next-step suggestions:

- low magnitude + low coherence
- high magnitude + low coherence
- low magnitude + higher coherence
- high magnitude + higher coherence

This add-on is intended for accessibility, biological follow-up planning, and non-statistical communication. It is not a clinical decision tree, validated responder classifier, or mechanistic validation workflow.
